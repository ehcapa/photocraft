The poselandmark.onnx weights in this directory are an ONNX export of MediaPipe's Pose
Landmarks Detector (BlazePose full, 33 body landmarks), taken from the community export
repository https://huggingface.co/fernandotonon/QtMeshEditor-poselandmarks-onnx
(Apache-2.0), itself a direct conversion of Google's MediaPipe (Apache-2.0,
https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe).
